## What Is an AI Writing App?

An AI writing app is a standalone application where you create, edit, or improve text.

Typically, the workflow looks like this:

1. Open the AI app.
2. Paste or type your text.
3. Enter a prompt.
4. Wait for the response.
5. Copy the result.
6. Return to your original app.
7. Paste the revised text.

Popular examples include:

- ChatGPT
- Grammarly
- Claude
- Jasper

These apps excel at producing long-form content, brainstorming ideas, summarizing information, or helping you start from a blank page.

If you're writing a blog post, report, or proposal, a dedicated AI writing app is often the best choice.

## What Is an AI Keyboard?

An AI keyboard works differently.

Instead of opening a separate app, the AI is built directly into your keyboard.

That means you can improve your writing anywhere you type, including:

- Messages
- Mail
- Notes
- Slack
- Discord
- LinkedIn
- X
- Reddit
- Safari
- Notion
- Almost any iPhone app

Rather than copying and pasting text between apps, you simply select your text, choose an AI command, and continue writing.

Common AI keyboard features include:

- Grammar correction
- Proofreading
- Paraphrasing
- Tone adjustments
- Translation
- Summarization
- Quick replies

The biggest difference isn't the AI model.

It's **where the AI lives**.

## The Hidden Cost of Copy-and-Paste

Standalone AI apps introduce small interruptions every time you edit text.

A typical workflow looks like this:

1. Leave your current app.
2. Open an AI writing app.
3. Paste your text.
4. Enter instructions.
5. Wait.
6. Copy the result.
7. Return.
8. Paste again.

It doesn't seem like much.

But repeat this process twenty or thirty times a day, and those extra steps become a significant productivity drain.

With an AI keyboard, the process is much simpler:

1. Select text.
2. Tap a rewrite command.
3. Replace the original.

Those saved seconds quickly add up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is an AI keyboard better than ChatGPT?

Not necessarily. ChatGPT is excellent for creating content from scratch, while an AI keyboard is designed to improve writing directly inside the apps you already use.

### Does an AI keyboard work in every app?

Most AI keyboards work anywhere the iPhone keyboard appears, including messaging apps, email clients, browsers, note-taking apps, and social media platforms.

### Can an AI keyboard replace Grammarly?

For many everyday writing tasks like grammar correction, rewriting, and tone adjustments, an AI keyboard can be an effective alternative. Dedicated grammar platforms may still offer additional document-level editing features.

### What is an AI rewrite app?

An AI rewrite app is a standalone application where users paste text to improve or paraphrase it. An AI keyboard performs similar tasks directly from the keyboard without requiring users to switch apps.

### Should I use both an AI writing app and an AI keyboard?

Yes. Many people use an AI writing app for brainstorming and long-form content while relying on an AI keyboard for emails, messages, and everyday communication.
