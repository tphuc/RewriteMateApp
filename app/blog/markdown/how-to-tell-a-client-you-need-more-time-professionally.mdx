# How to Tell a Client You Need More Time Professionally

Tell the client as soon as you know the original timing is at risk. State the current status, explain only the relevant reason, offer a realistic revised date or choice, and say what happens next. Do not wait until the deadline has passed, blame your workload, or promise a date you have not checked.

Early, specific communication gives a client more room to plan. The Freelancers Union likewise recommends notifying a client as much in advance as possible when extra time is needed. [Its guidance on deadline extensions](https://blog.freelancersunion.org/2014/10/08/what-say-when-you-need-deadline-extension/) is a helpful starting point.

![Professional Client Communication](https://res.cloudinary.com/diq74hzgo/image/upload/rewritemate/20260807_1629.jpg)

## Use this four-part structure

1. **Say what has changed.** Name the deliverable and original timing.
2. **Give a brief relevant reason.** Focus on work or a dependency, not a long personal explanation.
3. **Offer a realistic path.** Give a revised date, a partial delivery, or a decision the client can make.
4. **Confirm the next check-in.** State when you will update them.

## A professional template

> Hi [Name], I am writing about [deliverable], which we planned for [original date]. [One relevant reason] means I need additional time to complete it properly. I can deliver the completed [deliverable] by [realistic new date], or send [partial deliverable] by [earlier date] if that helps your planning. Please let me know which option works best; I will update you on [date].

Replace every bracket before sending. If you do not yet know a defensible new delivery date, do not invent one—promise an update after you have confirmed the plan.

## Examples for common situations

### You found work that needs correction

> Hi Maya, I am reviewing the website copy planned for Friday. During the final check, I found sections that need revision for consistency. I can send the completed version on Monday, or share the finished homepage copy Friday and the remaining pages Monday. Which option is more useful for your launch plan?

### You are waiting on information from the client

> Hi Maya, I am ready to complete the proposal once I have the final pricing information. Without it, I cannot verify the recommendations accurately. If you can send it by Wednesday, I can deliver the proposal by Friday; otherwise, I will confirm a revised timeline once it arrives.

### The date has already passed

> Hi Maya, I should have updated you before the agreed date for the campaign brief. The draft needs more work than I expected, and I do not want to send an incomplete version. I can send the revised brief by Tuesday at 3 PM. I will share a progress update tomorrow morning.

Own the communication lapse plainly. Avoid vague wording such as “things came up” or “I have been busy.”

## What not to say

| Avoid | Use instead |
| --- | --- |
| “I am swamped.” | “I need additional time to complete [specific work].” |
| “It will be done soon.” | “I can deliver by [date/time] after confirming [dependency].” |
| “The delay is not my fault.” | “Here is the current status and the next available path.” |
| “Sorry for the inconvenience.” only | Acknowledge the effect, then offer a concrete option. |

An apology matters when you missed a commitment. It does not replace a plan.

## Make the new timeline believable

Before offering a revised date, check the remaining work, review steps, dependencies, and any client approval you still need. A second missed deadline damages trust more than a carefully chosen first extension. If the client needs a result sooner, offer a partial delivery only when it is genuinely usable.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I tell a client I need more time?

Tell them as soon as you identify a real risk to the agreed date, ideally while they still have options to adjust their plan.

### Should I explain why the project is delayed?

Give enough relevant context to make the proposed plan understandable. Avoid unnecessary personal detail or blame.

### What if I cannot give a new date yet?

Say what you are checking and commit only to a realistic time for your next update.

### Is it better to offer a partial delivery?

Offer one only if it is useful to the client and will not create more rework or confusion.
