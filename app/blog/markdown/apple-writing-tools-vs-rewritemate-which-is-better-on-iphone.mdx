## What Are Apple Writing Tools?

Apple Writing Tools are part of **Apple Intelligence**, Apple's personal intelligence system available on supported iPhone, iPad, and Mac devices.

Rather than replacing your keyboard, Writing Tools appear when you select text inside compatible apps.

You can quickly:

- Proofread text
- Rewrite sentences
- Make writing friendlier
- Make writing more professional
- Make text more concise
- Summarize long passages

For many users, these built-in tools eliminate the need to copy and paste text into separate AI apps.

Because they're integrated into iOS, the experience feels familiar and requires almost no learning curve.

If your writing needs are occasional—replying to emails, editing notes, or polishing messages—Apple Writing Tools provide a convenient solution without installing additional software.

## Keyboard Experience

One of the biggest differences is **how** you interact with AI.

### Apple Writing Tools

Apple Writing Tools are not a keyboard.

Typical workflow:

3. Open Writing Tools.
4. Choose an option.
