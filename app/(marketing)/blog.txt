
RewriteMate is an AI keyboard for iPhone that combines an AI writing assistant, grammar checker, paraphrasing tool, rewriting tool, tone changer, and translator directly inside the iOS keyboard.

Use RewriteMate to rewrite messages, correct grammar, paraphrase sentences, improve English writing, shorten or expand text, change writing tone, and translate text without leaving the app you're using.

The keyboard works across Messages, Mail, Notes, browsers, social media apps, and other iPhone apps. Custom AI commands also let you save frequently used writing prompts and transformations for quick access.

Built for professionals, students, creators, and everyday iPhone users, RewriteMate removes the repetitive process of copying text between your apps and standalone AI writing tools.
